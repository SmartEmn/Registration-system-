Help Center

How do I register?

Open Register, enter your full name, grade, section, FAN and password, then submit the form.

What is FAN?

FAN means Fayda Alias Number. Handle this personal identifier carefully and only use an authorized verification method.

How do I log in?

Enter your FAN and the password associated with your account.

If you need account assistance, contact your school administrator.

About Us

The Student Portal is designed to make student registration and account access easier.

Students can enter their information, select their grade and section, and use their FAN when the secure registration system is ready.
